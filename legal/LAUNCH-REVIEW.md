# Zomeal legal release review — 10 September 2026

Business details and commercial promises were supplied by the owner. These documents are operational drafts, not a legal opinion or certification of compliance.

## Before public launch

- Replace the temporary Zomeal Grievance Team label with the required named grievance officer and designation; confirm the complete registered postal address. Do not invent a person, CIN, GSTIN or licence number.
- Confirm Zomeal's own applicable central FSSAI licence, not only provider registration/licensing. Listing approval is not a substitute for this.
- Have Indian counsel review marketplace, consumer, privacy, tax and payment arrangements. In particular, obtain payment-provider/legal advice on the stored meal balance and withdrawal structure; do not describe it as an RBI-licensed wallet without verification.
- Confirm the operational ability to return unused paid funds in 3–7 working days, reconcile pending charges, avoid double charges, and handle wrong/missed/unsafe meals. This change publishes policies and contacts, not a new automated withdrawal service.
- Confirm how promotional/referral funds are treated on withdrawal before advertising them as cash-withdrawable. These drafts promise withdrawal of customer-paid unused funds and do not invent a forfeiture rule.
- Review actual processor contracts, hosting regions, retention schedule and current data-protection commencement requirements. Do not promise a deletion deadline or fixed retention period that has not been operationally established.
- Confirm versioned acceptance and re-consent requirements for material terms changes in onboarding. Displaying policy text alone does not record acceptance.

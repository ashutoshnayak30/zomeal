# BIKI practice kitchen

Entry: floating BIKI button > Practice kitchen. Available locally to providers; no backend deployment needed.

15 rolling sample days, 100/200/500 customers, 10% paused and 5% cancelled. Active count is delivered for both lunch and dinner at Rs65/Rs55. All figures are illustrative; there is no repository or paid AI call in this screen.

Rice model: first closing check after day-one dinner; 14 subsequent days of deliveries, 80g per portion, twelve days' worth of purchases. User can enter a physical count and see the variance. No automatic adjustment is saved. Cooking expense assumption is 68% including gas; commission and overhead must be supplied explicitly. Full-month projections use the current month's calendar length and constant daily activity.

Closing this dialog resets its state. It does not seed customer accounts, orders, earnings, purchases or inventory. It is a training simulation, not an end-to-end backend or speech test.

Still outstanding for a full kitchen manager: owner-confirmed package conversions and aliases, persisted recipe/consumption calibration, waste/staff-meal capture, missing-purchase reconciliation, shopping recommendations, and cross-device settings. Never infer actual stock or book costs from delivery estimates alone.

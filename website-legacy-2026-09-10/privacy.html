Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

Information we collect

When you join early access, Zomeal collects your name, mobile number, pincode, optional locality, stated interest, consent time and submission time.

How we use it

We use this information to check launch interest, contact you about Zomeal availability, improve service-area planning and respond to provider enquiries. The website does not accept meal orders or payments.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell your contact information. Access is restricted to authorised Zomeal personnel and infrastructure providers needed to operate the service. We keep information only as long as reasonably required for these purposes and applicable legal obligations.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete your early-access information, or stop promotional contact, by writing to privacy@zomeal.in.

Security

We use access controls and moderated public provider data. No internet system is completely risk-free, and we continuously improve our safeguards.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact privacy@zomeal.in.

Plans and payments

Review the provider, meal slots, weekly or monthly duration, start date, price, fees and delivery address before confirming. The checkout amount is not necessarily the full plan price: an advance, meal-wallet credit and amounts already consumed are different. Your transaction record and chosen payment arrangement determine how money is applied; the same meal must not be charged twice. A saved unpaid selection is not an active subscription.

Wallet-funded meals

For wallet-funded service, eligible delivered lunch and dinner are charged at the applicable package rates. A balance insufficient for a meal can pause service until recharge and resumption. A low-balance warning is not a charge. Check your current schedule after recharging. Account history records payments and adjustments; report discrepancies to support.

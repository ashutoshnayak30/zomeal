GOOD TO KNOW

Before your first tiffin.

Can I start with only one week?+

Yes. Where a provider offers a weekly plan, you can use it to try the service before reviewing longer options.

Can I choose only lunch or dinner?+

Available options depend on the provider. A provider may offer lunch only, dinner only or a combined plan.

Can I pause a meal?+

Eligible meals can be paused before the relevant cut-off shown in the app. Confirmed late meals may still be chargeable.

Can I subscribe on this website?+

Not yet. The website helps you discover availability; subscriptions, wallets and meal management remain inside the Android app.

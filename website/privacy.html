Privacy Policy

Updated 18 November 2026 · Memobox Techno Lab Pvt Ltd

Scope and contact

Memobox Techno Lab Pvt Ltd operates Zomeal’s website, customer app and Zomeal-provider app, including the BIKI kitchen assistant. For privacy questions contact the Zomeal Grievance Team at support@zomeal.in, +91 6370648872, or Rairangpur, Mayurbhanj, Odisha – 757043, India.

Data we handle

Depending on your use, we handle your name, phone number, delivery address and pincode, account identifiers, meal preferences, subscriptions, order and wallet records, payment references, referrals, reviews, support messages and notification device tokens. Provider records can include business and contact details, service areas, menu and kitchen photos, food licences and settlement information. Website enquiries include the information and consent you submit. Technical logs may include device, network and error information.

Purposes

We use relevant data to authenticate accounts, check serviceability, fulfil meals, process and reconcile payments, manage referrals, send service alerts, resolve complaints and prevent fraud. We use enquiry details for the availability and testing updates requested. Promotional contact choices can be withdrawn without withdrawing a valid refund request.

Who receives data

Authorised Zomeal staff and service partners receive data needed for their roles. Your selected provider and allocated delivery personnel receive the contact, address and order details needed to fulfil delivery. Our infrastructure and authentication services include Supabase and SMS delivery services; Firebase Cloud Messaging handles push delivery and Razorpay processes payments. We may disclose records when lawfully required. We do not sell personal information.

BIKI kitchen assistant and voice processing

BIKI helps providers prepare kitchen records such as purchases, opening and closing stock, recipes, serving quantities and ingredient-cost estimates. Depending on the features available to your account, we process the kitchen information you enter or choose to record, including ingredient names, quantities, units, prices, serving rules, selected language and the resulting draft entries.

Optional microphone use

Voice entry is optional. The app requests microphone permission and presents a voice-processing choice before you use cloud voice entry. Recording starts when you choose to record; BIKI does not continuously listen in the background. You can use manual entry instead, withdraw voice consent in BIKI's voice privacy settings, or revoke microphone permission in Android settings. Your voice consent and language preference are remembered for your account on that phone.

Where audio and transcripts go

When you use cloud voice entry, the recorded audio is sent to our Google Cloud Run voice service and Google Cloud Speech-to-Text for transcription. The transcript is sent to Google Vertex AI to extract structured kitchen entries and, where needed, translate the transcript and ingredient labels into your selected supported language, including Odia. The current voice-processing flow sends audio for speech recognition in Singapore and processes transcripts for extraction and translation in India. These transfers occur even if you later discard the draft.

Supabase verifies account access and records voice-usage quota information, including request identifiers and recording duration, to manage access and usage limits. The voice-processing response is a draft: review names, quantities, units and costs before saving. Voice processing does not itself book an expense, change a wallet balance or save a stock count.

Storage and your choices

The Zomeal voice backend processes raw audio and transcripts for the request and does not write them to its application database or application logs. The app displays the transcript and draft for review. This is not a promise of zero retention throughout the cloud infrastructure: hosting and processing services handle operational and security records under their applicable service terms and configurations.

Kitchen entries you confirm and save are separate from the raw recording. Saved expenses, stock records, recipes and serving rules may remain in your kitchen account, cloud database or local app storage according to the feature you use. Discarding a voice draft does not delete previously saved records. Withdrawing voice consent stops future use of that optional processing; it does not automatically delete existing kitchen records.

Only record information needed for your kitchen task. Do not include customer contact details, passwords, OTPs, payment credentials or unrelated conversations. For access, correction or deletion of saved BIKI information, contact support@zomeal.in and identify your registered account and the records concerned. See our account-deletion instructions for deleting an account and associated data, subject to the retention limits below.

Payment and permissions

Enter sign-in OTPs only in the app's authentication flow; do not send OTPs, UPI PINs, card PINs or passwords to support or speak them into BIKI. Customer payment credentials are entered through the payment provider. Providers may submit bank account or UPI payout details for settlement. Notification permission is optional and can be changed in device settings; disabling it may mean you miss service alerts and does not necessarily stop notification identifiers from being generated. Photos or other device permissions are used for features you choose. Entering an address does not require continuous location tracking.

Storage, cookies and security

The website and apps may use local storage or similar technology for sessions and preferences; hosting and service providers process technical requests. Third-party resources such as website fonts can receive network information. Access controls and other safeguards reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security. Service providers may process data outside India subject to applicable legal requirements.

Retention and your choices

We retain records only as needed for service, security, disputes and applicable legal obligations. Request access, correction, consent withdrawal or deletion through support using your registered contact. We may reasonably verify identity. Deletion may be limited for payment, tax, fraud or dispute records that must be retained; we will explain any relevant limits. Optional marketing can be stopped separately.

Children, deletion and changes

The service is intended for adult account holders. Contact us if a child’s information was supplied without appropriate authority. See Account Deletion for the request process. We will publish policy updates and communicate material changes where appropriate. This notice does not restrict rights available under applicable data-protection law.
